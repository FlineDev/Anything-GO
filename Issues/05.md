# Weaknesses & disadvantages in Pokemon GO

**Boring**
- Not much to do when staying at one location for a long time (like at home / school / work)
- It's unfair since some people have Pokestops / Gyms nearby their home/work/school

**Hardware**
- Keeping the app open all the time + Using GPS all the time drains the battery a lot
- Steps aren't counted, so walking around at home / with bad connection doesn't help hatching
- Other devices that count steps (Apple Watch / Pebble / Others) aren't recognized

**Random**
- It is hard to decide for a team at the beginning, decision feels random
- Visiting new stops doesn't have any advantage, the received items are always random

**Cheating the goals**
- It's enough to just drive/walk by a location (PokeStop), without holding there and discovering it
- People with a bike (and car) have an easier time hatching since only kilometers count

**Locations**
- Places with gyms don't always provide any possibilities to rest (like seats)
- At some places the reception is really bad for many players, still stops/gyms/rare Pokemon

**Commercialized**
- Spending money helps hatching more eggs
- Spending money helps making double progress (lucky egg)
- Spending money helps finding more Pokemon (incense / lure module)
- Spending money helps taking more Pokemon / Items with you

**Social**
- There's no real advantage in being in a group and helping others in group
- There's no way to connect to players nearby and communicate within the game
- There's no way to compare with friends and see their progress without meeting and asking them
- There's no way of direct battle against a friend
