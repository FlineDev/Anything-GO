# Categorized conceptual regroup of Pokémon GO elements

This is a continuation of #1 and attempts to both categorize and regroup all elements found in #1.

**From a motivational perspective:**
- Motivation to **Start** playing the game:
  - Want to become a good Pokémon Trainer (be the user type and dive into the world)
- Motivation to **Move** (go outside):
  - Earns Points (find Pokestops, fight gyms, catch Pokemon)
  - Brings Star Powder to power up Pokémon (by Catching Pokemon)
  - Brings candy to evolve Pokémon
  - Pokémon appear randomly at any location (except for some due to respect/danger)
  - Nearby Pokémon are shown in radar --> gotta move to catch one you want
  - A given amount of distance has to be walked / ridden by a bike to hatch eggs
- Motivation to **Discover** (new places / information):
  - Additional information on collected content (learn more about the franchise/community)
  - Different Pokémon appear at different locations more often/regularly
  - Pokéstops are points of interest (currently uncategorized, no additional info, no need to stay)
  - Gyms are points of interest, too (currently doesn't matter if there are opportunities to sit)
  - Visiting Pokéstops brings all kind of items needed for the whole game (random right now)
- Motivation to **Socialize** (with other players):
  - Gyms can be occupied by multiple players of a team (only 1 Pokemon per player allowed)
  - Gyms can be fought together with other players for a better chance to win
  - Lure modules can be placed at Pokestops which attracts other players (as everybody profits)
- Motivation to **Keep** playing:
  - Increase the Trainer Level to become a better Trainer (Goal: Being the best)
  - Fill the Pokédex (Goal: Collect them all)
  - Get all badges in Gold Form (Goal: Mini-trohpies for different things)
  - Others from same team needing support (meaning the chosen group at Level 5 by "team")
  - Direct comparison indicators (Level, Pokedex, Badges)
  - Hatching eggs keeps the change to get all Pokémon even when not traveling much

From a **conceptual perspective** (or "what we need to do"):

**Note that examples for Harry Potter (HP) are given. The solution in Pokémon GO (PK) is referenced for better understanding.**
- The game has to have **clear goals**:
  - PK:
    - **Catch** all Pokemon
    - **Train** your Pokemon to beat all others
    - Collect **Badges**
    - Defend **Gyms**
  - HP: 
    - Become master for all **spells**
    - Become master for all **potions**
    - **Train quidditch** to become the best player at your position
    - Collect **badges**
- Motivation to **Start** playing the game:
  - Make sure the **game experience relates** to something the community always **wished** to be a part of
    - PK: Become a Pokémon trainer and "catch em' all"
    - HP: Be a witch or wizard and go to Hogwarts school of witchcraft and wizardry
  - Introduce **minimum progress level** before fighting is possible
    - PK: Gyms can be fighted at beginning at level 5
    - HP: Spells cannot be cast until first semester over / starts
      - also before school year starts there could be an introduction using the "buying ceremony"
- Motivation to **Move** (go outside):
  - Introduce a **progress tracker system** where each action that serves the goals helps progressing
    - PK: Trainer experience points with different Levels that can be reached
    - HP: Magical experience points with different school years at distinct subjects
      - Those subjects could be: Defense ag. the dark arts, potions, etc. + Quidditch
  - Introduce **main collection/battling content** to be collected for battling against others
    - PK: Pokémon appear randomly at any location and can be captured (with skill)
      - multiple of the same Pokémon can be caught, usually only the strongest kept
      - Pokémon have types that are more / less effective against each other
      - Catching a Pokemon brings both candy and star powder
    - HP: Masters of a specific subject are met at any location and can be learned from (with skill)
      - meeting a character multiple times increases the friendship with that character
      - each character teaches one spell that can be learned (first time) or improved (other times)
      - each training brings the player closer to becoming a master of the spell (spell strength)
      - a character teaches any spell of his kind (randomly) –- multiple characters can be a spells master
        - e.g. Snape teaches different potions spells, could also Defense ag. dark arts – has two types
        - training a spell brings both spell specific instructions (homework?) and chocolate frogs
          - funny details: Snape doesn't give any chocolate frogs, more instructions though
      - spells have strategic concepts behind them (read on for more)
    - Note: While Pokemon are _met_ AND _caught_ in Pokémon GO, those are two different concepts
  - Introduce **resources for improving the fighting strength** of the main collection content
    - PK: Star Powder & Pokémon specific Candies – needed to make Pokémon stronger
    - HP: Spell specific instructions & Chocolate frogs – can be read to further improve a spell and eaten while learning
  - Introduce **jump levels in fighting strength** on main collection content
    - PK: Pokemon can be evolved once, twice or not at all and "jump" in their strength
    - HP: All spells have different control levels
      - e.g.: When learning the spell "Wingardium Leviosa"
        - Beginner: Learned the words for a spell (after first collecting it)
        - Advanced: Mastered the correct movement/thoughts/concentration for spell
        - Pro: Mastered the spell nonverbally
  - Introduce concept to **find main collectable content nearby** (so players have to move)
    - PK: Nearby Pokemon are shown with steps of their distance
    - HP: Marauder's Map shows the teachers nearby with their direction and distance
  - Introduce concept to collect **(rare) random** main collectable content **by walking distances**
    - PK: A given amount of distance has to be walked / ridden by a bike to hatch eggs
      - A hatched egg gives the same things as when a Pokemon is caught in the wild
      - Additionally star powder and candy are given more + 500 Points are received
    - HP: Chocolate frogs boxes can be filled with magic by walking / riding by bike
      - Once the magic is filled on a chocolate frog, the frog jumps out
      - The chocolate frog is one kind of currency that can be collected (instead of star powder)
      - Theres a white chocolate frog box which is similar to rarer eggs (10km-eggs)
      - Chocolate frog cards can be collected (similar to the Pokedex)
      - Each chocolate frog box also includes special instructions on spells
- Motivation to **Discover** (new places / information):
  - Additional **information on collected content** (learn more about the franchise/community)
    - PK: Each Pokemon has a Pokedex entry
    - HP: Each Chocolate Frog Card provides some information about a known witch/wizard
  - Introduce a **rarity** for different main collection content and **different places**
    - PK: Different Pokémon appear at different locations more often/regularly
      - Some Pokemon are only available in a different continent
      - Some Pokemon are only available in a big event with many players (Mewtwo)
    - HP: Different chocolate frog cards appear in different locations more often/regularly
      - Some chocolate frog cards only appear in different regions
      - Some frog cards can only be received by defeating a deatheater attack
  - Introduce points of interest where **important items of different kind** can be **collected**
    - PK: Pokéstops are points of interest (uncategorized, no additional info, no need to stay)
    - HP: Port keys are points of interest (the school subject)
      - Visiting a port key earns chocolate frog boxes, additional spell instructions, more
- Motivation to **Socialize** (with other players):
  - Introduce concept of **different groups** a player can be part of
    - PK: Players choose either Team Red, Blue or Yellow and can't change for their lifetime
    - HP: Players are sorted into one of the houses Gryffindor, Ravenclaw, Hufflepuff or Slytherin
      - the sorting ceremony is the step that really makes the school year begin
      - for sorting the sorting hat asks 4 questions, the player has to make choices
      - an additional question is asked and allows player to exclude one house
  - Introduce points of interest for **battling against other players** in some way
    - PK: Gyms are points of interest for battling 
      - currently doesn't matter if there are opportunities to sit
      - battling is done asynchronously against defending Pokemon
      - more people increase the chance to win when battling at the same time
    - HP: Quidditch pitches are points of interest for battling
      - there should nearly always be opportunities to sit
      - pitches are empty at the beginning, the first house to visit the pitch with 7 players gets it first
      - if no game is started in a pitch, the defending team holds it
      - a game starts at a pitch the moment the seventh player from the same house visits
      - while a game is in progress, up to 7 people nearby can attack / defend
      - each player has a different role, communicates with other players in team
      - the gameplay mechanisms have to be established, basics: quaffle, pludger, snitch appear
      - battling is done continually, a game is won when the snitch is caught
      - everybody from the same team can defend / attack together
      - a new match starts after 7 players of the same team visited place, 7th player leads the team
      - more players should increase the chance to win
  - Introduce **item everybody profits from** to get people together at specific locations
    - PK: Lure modules can be placed at Pokestops which attracts Pokemon for everyone
    - HP: Fiests can be organized at class rooms / tournaments at pitches
      - TODO: details have yet to be established
- Motivation to **Keep** playing:
  - Centrally show **player overall progress** increased on every goal-serving action
    - PK: Increase the Trainer Level to become a better Trainer (Goal: Being the best)
    - HP: Magical Experience with Classes to walk through (Goal: Mastering school)
  - Centrally show **main collection content** progress
    - PK: Fill the Pokédex (Goal: Collect them all)
    - HP: Get all chocolate frog cards
  - Centrally show **different statistics** reached
    - PK: Get all badges in Gold Form (Goal: Mini-trohpies for different things)
    - HP: Get all trophies in Gold Form (Goal: Mini-trohpies for different things)
  - Direct **comparison indicators** with other players / friends to talk about
    - PK: Level of player, Pokemon in Pokedex, collected Badges
    - HP: Class and semester of player, Card collection, Hall of Fame
      - A leader board amongst people in the same region, country and world
      - "you are amongst the 12% best this month" --> show best statistic from all
